About us

The degree is the beginning. The network is the multiplier.

Women across Africa are earning MBAs in growing numbers. Too often the qualification arrives without the sponsorship, capital and connections that turn it into influence. MBA Women in Africa exists to close that gap.

Our mission

A serious professional home for African women in business education.

We are a pan-African community of more than 1,200 women in over 15 countries. Our lens is continental and our framing is MBA-specific: strategy, finance and leadership, applied to the markets we actually work in.

Membership is by application. That is a deliberate choice. It keeps the room focused, the conversations honest and the introductions valuable for everyone in it.

We are building towards an institution: a member platform, a mentorship programme, and in time a fund that invests in the women of this community.

What we stand for

Our values

Rooted

Proudly African and pan-African. We build for East, West and Southern Africa and the diaspora, never one country alone.

Rising

Ambitious without apology. We celebrate the promotion, the raise, the board seat and the first investment.

Relentless

Practical over performative. Every programme has to move a member's career, business or wealth forward.

Meet the founder

Nosipho Dinwa

Nosipho has spent more than fifteen years in learning, leadership development and hospitality, building programmes that help people grow, from front-line teams to senior leaders. Her career has taken her from Walt Disney World to national and global learning leadership roles.

She founded MBA Women in Africa in 2025 while completing her own MBA, after seeing how much talent sat in lecture halls without the networks to match. She also leads mentorship across the continent as Director of Training, Mentorship and Education at the African Association of Women in Tourism and Hospitality.

  • MBA candidate, UCT Graduate School of Business
  • Co-Chair, UCT GSB Women in Business
  • AAWTH executive committee
  • Hospitality faculty advisory boards

Our community agreement

  • What is shared here, stays here. No screenshots outside the workspace.
  • Lead with generosity. Answer the question you once needed answered.
  • Ask boldly. There is no small question in a room of peers.
  • No unsolicited selling. Offers belong in #partner-offers.
  • Respect every voice. We are many countries, languages and paths.
